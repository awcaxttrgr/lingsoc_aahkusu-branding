Colour & Placement Guidelines

Refer to Visual Identity Manual for details.

Do not change colour/tint/fade
Monochrome can be recoloured
Use Greyscale for B&W print
Ensure sufficient contrast between logotype and background
Do not place logotype on busy backgrounds
Use Negative variant against basic brand colours for contrast
Ensure logotype is visible and doesn't overpower the photo

Logotype Usage Rules

Refer to Visual Identity Manual for details.

Do not extend, crop or modify
Do not compress or expand
Do not extrapolate
Do not create new lock-ups
Do not recreate the logotype
Do not change typeface
Do not replace the Globe
Do not outline
Do not apply effects
Do not invade clear space
Keep clear space on page
Do not use low-res images
