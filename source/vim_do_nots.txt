LOGOTYPE - WHAT NOT TO DO

[Rules to be displayed statically]

GENERIC RULES
Do not extend, crop or in any way modify the logotype.  img\vim_rules_do_not_modify_1.png, img\vim_rules_do_not_modify_2.png

Do not compress or expand the logotype. You must always retain the original proportions when resizing the logotype.  img\vim_rules_do_not_distort_1.png, img\vim_rules_do_not_distort_2.png

Do not extrapolate from the logotype. Supplementary text must be placed outside of the clear space around the logotype. img\vim_rules_do_not_extrapolate.png

Do not create new lock-ups.     img\vim_rules_do_not_create_new_lockups.png

Do not recreate the logotype.   img\vim_rules_do_not_recreate.png

Do not use your own typeface to recreate the logotype.  img\vim_rules_do_not_change_typeface.png

Do not replace the Globe with any other visual identity element.   img\vim_rules_do_not_change_logo.png

APPEARANCE (COLOUR AND EFFECTS)
Do not change the colour, tint or fade of the logotype, except the Monochromatic variant. img\vim_rules_do_not_change_colour.png, img\vim_rules_do_not_change_colour_monochrome.png

Do not outline the logotype. img\vim_rules_do_not_outline.png

Do not apply any effect on the logotype, such as bevel, glow, and drop shadow. img\vim_rules_do_not_add_effects.png

Do not print the Standard logotype in black and white. Use the Greyscale variant.   img\vim_rules_do_not_change_colour_bw.png

DESIGN
Do not add anything (including text) in the clear space.    img\vim_rules_do_not_invade_clearspace.png

Do not place the logotype too close to the margin. Make sure the clear space stays on the page. img\vim_rules_do_not_invade_clearspace_margin.png

Do not use low-quality versions of the logotype. Graphic designers should always use the vector image of the logotype.  img\vim_rules_do_not_use_low_res.png

[Rules to be displayed by cycling through listed images]

PLACEMENT
Do not use the Standard lock-up on a dark background, or the Negative variant lock-up on a light background. Ensure enough contrast between the logotype and the background.

When placing the logo over a background photo or other element, make sure the logotype is clearly visible and doesn’t get overpowered by the background. This may mean choosing a different photo, using the Negative variant or repositioning the logotype so it rests on a less busy area of the photo. Also be aware of not covering up a critical area of the photo.

Use the negative variant of the logotype when it is set against one of the basic colours. Do not use the primary logotype against any of the basic colours. This would create too less contrast between the logotype and the background.
